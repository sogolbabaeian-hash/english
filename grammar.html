English Grammar

Present Simple

I study English.

Present Continuous

I am learning English.

Past Simple

I watched a movie.

Grammar Example
Present Simple I play.
Present Continuous I am playing.
Past Simple I played.

More Examples

I read books.

She is watching TV.

They played football.

Grammar Rules

Present Simple برای کارهای همیشگی
Present Continuous برای کارهای در حال انجام
Past Simple برای کارهای گذشته
